📜 Welcome to GolViral

We built GolViral to be lightweight, fast globally via Cloudflare, with secure cloud database and encrypted media storage. Payments for digital features are processed securely via Selar.

🔒 Privacy Policy

FOR YOU FEED • TIKTOK-STYLE ALGORITHM

🔥 How GolViral Algorithm Works - Maximum Reach

GolViral uses a For You recommendation system designed for maximum organic reach, not just followers. Even with 10 followers, your reel or image can go viral if people watch and engage.

How For You Feed Ranks Content

  • Watch Time & Completion: Full watch and re-watches = more reach.
  • Engagement Velocity: Likes, comments, shares to DM and follows in the first 30-60 minutes push your post to more users.
  • Interest Graph: We show content based on interests, hashtags, captions and viewing history, not only who you follow.
  • Recency + Relevance: Fresh posts get initial test audience. If test group engages, it expands.
  • Deduplicated Views: One user counts once per day per post for fairness.
  • Quality Signals: Original reels, clear images, good captions and no spam lead to higher distribution.

How to Get Maximum Reach

  • Hook in first 2 seconds: Start with action or question.
  • Post Consistently: Daily reels + images + stories = faster growth.
  • Use Hashtags + Captions: We index captions and hashtags to match to interested viewers.
  • Engage Back: Reply to comments and DM quickly.
  • Boost Option: Use Boost for 1, 3 or 7 days for priority placement.
  • Mix Formats: Use reels for discovery, images for retention, stories for connection, novels for loyal readers.

PAYMENTS, EARNINGS & PAYOUT SCHEDULE

💳 Payments, Earnings and Payout Schedule

Two Balances - Simple

  • Credit Balance - For Everyone (Free Credit Personal Account): Used for posting, commenting, following, chatting. You get credits daily, from welcome bonus, watching content and engagement. This balance cannot be withdrawn. All accounts start here by default.
  • Earnings Balance - For Monetized Creators Only: Unlocked only after you complete all monetization requirements (10 followers + 7 days + face verification + quality + system checks). This balance is withdrawable.

First Withdrawal - Government ID Required

For security and to prevent fraud, your first withdrawal requires a valid Government ID.

  • Accepted: National ID, International Passport, Voter's Card, Driver's License, or any government-issued ID.
  • Document image is reviewed once and deleted within 24 hours after verification.
  • After first withdrawal is approved, future withdrawals only need face verification + email OTP, no ID again unless flagged for security.

Payout Schedule - Twice Monthly

  • GolViral pays out twice every month: 1st - 3rd and 15th - 17th of every month.
  • Request must be made before payout window. Requests during payout window will be processed in next window.
  • Minimum: ₦5,000 = 50,000 credits for local bank, $10 = 120,000 credits and $100 = 1,200,000 credits for USDT / USD.
  • Methods: Local Bank Transfer and USDT TRC20 / USD.
  • Only one pending request at a time. Withdrawals are reviewed and paid directly by GolViral.

How You Earn (After Monetization)

  • Gifts: When fans buy Ruby, Gold, Diamond via Selar and send to your content, you earn.
  • Engagement: Qualified views (1 per user per day), likes, comments on original content.
  • Referrals: When your invited friend complete face verification process, you earn referral bonus.

Purchases via Selar

All gift and boost purchases are processed securely via Selar. Upon confirmation, credits are added automatically. Contact support@golviral.com with Selar reference within 7 days if payment succeeded but feature not delivered.

✅ Verification - Two Steps, Clear

Step 1: Face Verification - To Unlock Monetization

Required after 10 followers + 7 days. One-time liveness selfie. This unlocks Earnings and makes your referrals valid. Image deleted within 24 hours.

Step 2: Government ID - To Unlock First Withdrawal

Required only for first withdrawal. You must provide any valid Government ID (National ID, Passport, Voter's Card, Driver's License). This is to confirm you are the account owner and prevent fraud. Document deleted within 24 hours after review.

Example: If you have 5,000 followers but you have not completed face verification, you remain on Free Credit Personal Account and cannot earn yet. If you complete face verification but have not submitted Government ID, you can earn and see Earnings Balance but you cannot withdraw until Government ID is verified. Both steps are required for payout.

Verification Failure

Using another person's face, using same face for multiple accounts, using filters, or submitting fake ID will lead to verification failure and account may remain on Free Credit Personal Account or be restricted.
