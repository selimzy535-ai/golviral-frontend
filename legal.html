📜 Welcome to GolViral

GolViral.com is an installable Progressive Web App (PWA) that lets you create, discover and monetize content. By using our website, PWA, APIs hosted on golviral-api.onrender.com, and related services, you agree to the policies on this page.

Our mission is simple: give creators the fastest path to being discovered and earning from what they love doing. Whether you post a 15-second reel, a photo carousel, a written story or a short novel chapter, your content can be found by people who care.

We built GolViral to be lightweight, fast globally via Cloudflare, with reliable database powered by Neon and media storage and streaming via Backblaze. Payments for digital items are processed securely via Selar.

Your wallet has two distinct balances: Credit Balance for platform actions like posting and messaging, and Earnings Balance for monetization. This separation prevents confusion and spam.

FOR YOU FEED • TIKTOK-STYLE ALGORITHM

🔥 How GolViral Algorithm Works - Maximum Reach

GolViral uses a TikTok-style For You recommendation system designed for maximum organic reach, not just followers. Even with 10 followers, your reel or image can go viral if people watch and engage.

How For You Feed Ranks Content

  • Watch Time & Completion: The algorithm prioritizes how long users watch your reel. Full watch and re-watches = more reach. For images, dwell time and swipe-through rate matters.
  • Engagement Velocity: Likes, comments, shares to DM, saves and follows in the first 30-60 minutes push your post to more users.
  • Interest Graph, Not Just Social Graph: Like TikTok, we show content based on interests, hashtags, captions and viewing history, not only who you follow. A new account with 10 followers can reach thousands if content matches viewer interest.
  • Recency + Relevance: Fresh posts get initial test audience. If test group engages, it expands to larger audience.
  • Deduplicated Views: One user counts once per day per post for fairness and to prevent fake inflation.
  • Quality Signals: Original reels, clear images, good captions, no watermark from other apps, and no spam lead to higher distribution.

How to Get Maximum Reach on GolViral

  • Hook in first 2 seconds: For reels, start with action or question. Our algo measures early drop-off.
  • Post Consistently: Algorithm learns your niche. Daily reels + images + stories = faster growth.
  • Use Hashtags + Captions: We index captions and hashtags to match to interested viewers in scroll.
  • Engage Back: Reply to comments and DM quickly. Comments double your reach chance.
  • Boost Option: If you want guaranteed push, use Boost for 1, 3 or 7 days. Boosted posts get priority placement but still must pass review and get organic engagement to keep going.
  • Mix Formats: Our algorithm rewards creators who use all activities: reels for discovery, images for retention, stories for daily connection, novels for loyal readers, chat for community.

What Algorithm Does NOT Do

  • Does not shadowban for posting often. You can post multiple times daily if quality stays high.
  • Does not require you to pay to be seen. Organic reach is free and based on watch time and engagement.
  • Does not use precise GPS. We use only timezone and interest, not location tracking.
  • Does not sell your watch history. Your For You personalization stays on GolViral.

Fairness and Anti-Spam

To keep feed clean, GolViral uses free credit system and rate limits. Fake views, fake followers from multiple accounts, bots or view loops are detected via Neon data patterns and removed. Real engagement from real people always wins for maximum reach.

🎁 Gifts & Boost - Detailed Rules

Gifts

GolViral offers optional gift packs: Ruby, Gold and Diamond tiers. Gifts are a way for fans to support creators. When a user sends a gift on your reel or image, you receive a portion as earnings. Gifts are digital items with no cash value outside platform, except for creator earnings as defined in monetization.

Gift sending requires sufficient credit or direct purchase via Selar. Gift animations are shown publicly on post. Gifts have a validity period of 30 days for display purposes. Abuse such as self-gifting via multiple accounts to inflate earnings is prohibited and will be reversed.

Boost

Boost increases the reach of your chosen post. Available durations: 1 day, 3 days, 7 days. Boosted posts receive priority in For You and Explore feeds, higher placement in hashtag pages and increased profile visits. Boost does not guarantee followers, likes or earnings, only increased impressions.

Boosted content must comply with all Community Guidelines. No adult, hate, spam or misleading content can be boosted. We reserve the right to reject boost requests after system review. Boosted posts are labeled as Boosted for transparency.

Payments via Selar

All purchases of gifts and boost are processed securely via Selar. We do not store card numbers. After payment, you receive Selar reference and our system unlocks the feature. If technical failure prevents delivery and is confirmed by logs, contact support@golviral.com with Selar reference within 7 days for review. Digital sales are otherwise final.

✅ Verification System

Face Verification for Monetization

To unlock monetization after meeting 10 followers and 7 days old account age, you must complete a one-time face liveness check. This ensures one real person per account and prevents bots from farming referrals and earnings. Process: you take a selfie following prompts. Our system checks liveness. Image is stored temporarily for review and deleted within 24 hours. We do not retain biometric templates.

Document Verification for Withdrawal Security

For security, we may request document verification before first withdrawal to confirm identity and prevent fraud. Document image is also deleted after review within 24 hours. We do not share verification data with third parties except as required for fraud prevention.

Verification Abuse

Using another person's face, fake documents, filters that obscure identity, or attempting to verify multiple accounts with same face will result in verification failure and possible suspension.
