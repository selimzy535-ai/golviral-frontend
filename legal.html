🔒 Privacy Policy

What We Collect

  • Account info: username, email, encrypted password, bio, avatar
  • Social info: followers, following, likes, comments, views (deduplicated fairly to prevent spam)
  • Messages between creators (for DM feature)
  • Basic device timezone to show relevant content (no GPS location tracking)

What We Don't Do

  • We do NOT sell your personal data.
  • We do NOT use intrusive third-party trackers.
  • We do NOT store your biometric face template - verification is one-time authenticity check.

Storage & Security

Media is securely stored and streamed using enterprise cloud storage and CDN partners including Backblaze and Cloudflare. All data is encrypted in transit. We use secure email services for OTP and notifications.

Cookies

We only use localStorage/cookies for keeping you logged in and for PWA functionality. No advertising cookies for tracking. You can clear data in browser settings anytime.

Push Notifications

As a PWA, we may send push notifications for new followers, comments, and messages if you opt in. You can disable anytime in your browser or device settings. We use standard Web Push (VAPID).
