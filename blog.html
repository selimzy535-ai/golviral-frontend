GolViral is a short video, meme, photo, and short novel platform where creation and consumption are both rewarded. It was built to solve the biggest frustration for new creators on traditional platforms: you can get views but you get zero dollars until you hit huge follower milestones. On GolViral, monetization is not an afterthought. It is the foundation.

Every account starts with a wallet. Every post starts earning from the first legitimate view. There is no application to join a creator fund, no minimum followers to unlock earnings, no waiting list. If those signals are strong, your post is pushed to tier 2: 10k-25k views. If it continues performing, tier 3: 50k-88k+ and beyond. This means virality is not about who you know, it is about how long you keep people watching.

A view where someone watches 2 seconds and swipes is worth less than a view where someone watches to the end, likes, comments, and shares. To reward quality, we pay on a range. Low retention gets $8 end, high retention gets $15 end.
